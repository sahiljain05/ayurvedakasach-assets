About

Ayurveda Ka Sach publishes short (under 60 seconds) clips taken from the official long-form talks of Acharya Manish Ji. Each clip covers an everyday lifestyle topic — food combinations, daily routine (dinacharya), seasonal habits — and the caption lists the classical text reference and a published research paper behind it, with an honest note on what that study actually shows.

All content is educational. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with a qualified doctor. We do not publish claims of cures or guaranteed results.
