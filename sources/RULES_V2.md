# Clip rules v2 + 1-3 min reels (user approved, 26 Nov 23:02 IST)

Replaces the stricter parts of the clip compliance screen in daily_prompt.md, select_prompt.md, WEBINAR_MODE.md, LIVE_MODE.md and SOURCE_POOL.md for the Ayurveda Ka Sach channel (IG, FB, YT Shorts). VOPD ads and YT long videos keep their own rules.

## 1. Now ALLOWED (was rejected before)
- Naming a disease when the clip gives diet, routine, parhez or lifestyle guidance for it ("kidney ke mareez namak kaise kam karein", "sugar mein raat ka khana kab khayen", "BP mein kya na khayen", "liver ke liye kya khayen").
- Ayurveda's view of a disease/condition, its causes and prevention, in general terms.
- Acharya Ji's general answer to a patient's question in a webinar/live, if the patient's face, name and phone number are not shown or heard (cut or crop them out; audio of the question may be replaced by an on-screen text of the question).
- Strong, emotional, provocative hooks and opinions (e.g. "ye galti 90% log karte hain", "doctor ye nahi batayenge" style) as long as they are Acharya Ji's own words and not one of the 3 hard lines below.
- Burned-in ticker/phone number/promo strip if the crop removes it fully (check the contact sheet). Spoken phone numbers or "call now" promo still get cut.
- Webinars and lives that were skipped for being "disease webinars": re-scan them with these rules (live_videos.txt and webinar_log.md entries marked skipped/done 0 clips → set back to todo with a note "rescan v2").

## 2. HARD LINES (unchanged, never post)
1. Cure/guarantee claims for any disease: "theek ho jayega", "jad se khatm", "100%", "guarantee", "kabhi nahi hoga", "ilaaj" presented as a cure, reversing a disease.
2. Anything that tells or implies to stop, skip, reduce or avoid medicine, insulin, chemo, dialysis, biopsy, surgery or a doctor's treatment.
3. Patient testimonials or identifiable patients (face/name/number) without documented consent.
Also still banned: Dr. Biswaroop Roy Chowdhury footage/voice, hospital/package/price promos, specific herb dosages for a disease.
If a hard-line sentence sits inside an otherwise good window, cut that sentence cleanly (complete sentences before/after) or drop the window.

## 3. Reel length
- Allow 30-180 s reels (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts both take up to 3 min).
- Mix: about half 60-180 s (complete explanations, stories), half 30-60 s (one sharp point).
- Longer reels must hold attention: strong first 3 s, no dead air (trim pauses > 1 s), captions throughout, a text hook in the first 2 s, and end on a complete thought.
- Same 9:16 speaker-crop, face QC and caption style. Keep each file under 100 MB (Instagram/GitHub).

## 4. Captions/titles
- Hooks can be bold, but captions and titles must not add claims the clip doesn't make and must respect the hard lines. Keep the ⚠️ disclaimer line on IG/FB captions and YT descriptions.
