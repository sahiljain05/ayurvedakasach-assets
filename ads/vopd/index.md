## Deviations from SPEC.md (could not be done exactly as written)
1. **Voice-over does not fit 5 s for S1, S2 (male) and S3.** S1 was measured on all 30 hi-IN Chirp3-HD voices at the maximum
   speakingRate 1.1: 5.6–7.7 s (S2/S3/S4 on the 6 fastest voices: 4.6–5.8 s). Script words were kept exactly, rate stays
   at 1.1 max, pauses inside the VO were shortened to ≤ 0.18 s and the shortest of several takes was kept. Where the VO still
   does not fit, the endplate was lengthened just enough (VO starts at +0.1 s, ends ≥ 0.15 s before the reel ends):
   S1 (F, VO 5.62 s) → 5.9 s endplate; S3 (M 5.10 s / F 5.12 s) → 5.4 s; S2 (M 4.77 s) → 5.1 s; S4 (F 4.66 s) → 5.0 s.
   Total reel length stays 20–35 s. The VO was never sped up beyond rate 1.1 and never cut. (ENDPLATE_VO.md part 3 later
   allows endplates up to 7 s.)
2. **Ducking:** the clip audio is sidechain-compressed under the VO, but the source audio after each window is NOT continued
   under the endplate (in most sources the next sentence is the channel's "गंभीर बीमारियों का इलाज … पंचकर्म" promo or a disease
   claim). The body audio fades out over 0.12 s at the cut, so in practice the VO plays over silence.
3. **Pronunciation:** TTS input uses "हिम्स" for HiiMS and "जीना सीखो" for "Jeena Sikho" (same words, Devanagari spelling for
   correct pronunciation; on-screen text unchanged). No listen-check was possible.
4. **Line 2 of the card** is set on two lines ("Virtual OPD" / "घर बैठे डॉक्टर से वीडियो पर सलाह"); the "·" separator is the line break.
5. **👉 glyph** is the monochrome Noto Emoji font (OFL); the VM has no colour-emoji font that the renderer can draw.
6. **Sources:** only LmpEEPMpNLI was still on disk; the others were re-imported via vidIQ (free). vidIQ had 2 credits, so YouTube
   captions for compliance screening were fetched with yt-dlp (public auto-captions) instead of vidiq_video_transcript.
   Reel 15 (DmWr7zGSa84) comes from a 640x360 webinar source and looks soft when cropped to 1080x1920.
7. Several reels reuse tips the channel already posted (milk+fruit, water with meals, chewing, breakfast) — allowed for ads;
   no two ads share the same tip.
8. The newer endplate scripts in ENDPLATE_VO.md part 3 are NOT applied here (SPEC.md still lists S1–S4); the queued
   endplate-fix job will re-render these endplates. Body length per reel is in the Duration column (body = first N seconds).
